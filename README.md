Lawgic – AI-Powered Legal Research Assistant

**Lawgic** is a smart legal research platform that leverages AI to simplify legal analysis and access to Indian law. With features like a conversational legal chatbot, intelligent document reader, and IPC section finder, Lawgic makes navigating the legal landscape faster, more intuitive, and more accessible.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 💬 **Lawgic Research Chatbot** – Ask legal queries related to IPC, RTI, labor laws, etc., and get instant, contextual answers using RAG-based AI.
- 📄 **Lawgic DocReader** – Upload legal documents (judgments, FIRs, notices) and extract structured insights automatically.
- ⚖️ **Lawgic IPC Finder** – Instantly find relevant IPC sections based on keywords or case scenarios.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React.js  
- **Backend / Auth:** Firebase  
- **AI / ML:**
  - Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  - Gemini 1.5 Pro (fine-tuned)
  - Mistral-7B, BERT, LangChain
- **Python Libraries:** Streamlit, LangChain, FAISS, PyPDF2
- **Data Sources:** India Code, Legal CSV datasets (from Kaggle and public repositories)
